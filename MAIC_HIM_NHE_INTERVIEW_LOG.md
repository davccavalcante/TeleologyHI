## Glossary & framing note (audit 2026-05-18)

The interview was conducted in PT-BR over many sessions. The Creator's voice is preserved verbatim in the Portuguese blocks, while each `**Answer:**` in English is an interpretive synthesis. Two practical clarifications for any reader entering the log cold:
